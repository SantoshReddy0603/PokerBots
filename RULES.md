# Supplied rules (updated 10 November 2026)

The Rulebook governs, except the Problem Statement governs variant mechanics. Protocol 1.0 governs the wire format. Documents in new_updates supersede the earlier documents and prevail over the starter kit if it disagrees. Updated source hashes are in experiments/rules-update-1110/sources.json; original hashes remain in experiments/sources.csv.

- Python 3.11, standard library plus numpy and scipy, a top-level bot.py or ZIP no larger than 5 MB. Reading files within the submission is allowed; writing files, network, subprocesses and state transfer between legs/matches are forbidden. Our bot remains self-contained.
- Each match has two legs with fresh bot processes. Stage 1 has 200 hands per leg (400 total); Stage 2 has 500 per leg (1000 total). Each deal occurs once per leg, with hole cards and seats swapped in the other leg. No attempt to identify duplicate deals is allowed.
- Provisional limits are 1 CPU, 1 GB, 5 s per response, 30/75 s cumulative bank PER LEG and 90/225 s wall clock PER LEG. Each leg has 5 s startup grace: a request's clock starts at the later of request dispatch and launch + 5 s. A first reply can therefore arrive up to 10 s after launch.
- Reset stacks to 200 with blinds 1/2. Button acts first preflop and last afterward. At most four raises per street, counting the big blind. Raise amounts are street totals. Follow supplied legal bounds including short all-ins.
- Three private cards through flop betting, then a simultaneous exposed discard unless someone folded. All-in players still discard. Showdown uses the best five from the board and the retained two. Split ties.
- JSON lines, flush responses only to the two requests. init.total_hands is the leg length, hand_id restarts at 1 per leg, and time_left_ms is the leg bank. Exit after match_end at the end of each leg. Action requests omit private cards; discard requests omit history. Ignore unrecognized fields/types. Corrections apply check or fold, or lowest card by rank then s/h/d/c. No reply is a timeout, not a corrected discard. legal_actions is authoritative even when raises_left is positive (e.g. an all-in opponent).
- Match points 1/0.5/0 use net chips across BOTH legs, then summed capped whole-match deltas divided by scheduled hands. Caps remain 400/1000. Forfeit in either leg ends the whole match: loser -cap, winner +cap; simultaneous forfeit draws. A forfeit's denominator is scheduled hands (400/1000), never partial hands played. Replay determines unresolved prize/cutoff ties.
- Only a local leg score is visible to the bot. It cannot certify a whole-match lead. The selected bot already disables its old full-match protection for the official 200/500-hand leg lengths. An explicit score-scope/reset candidate is preserved separately after an unfavorable comparison; it is not the selected submission.
- No use of duplicate order, logs, seed, hidden cards, or future boards. The policy discards past hand cards at the hand boundary and resets learned state at init. No information passes between legs. Tournament/eligibility provisions remain in the supplied Rulebook.
- Original organizer files under vendor/Starter_Kit remain unchanged for historical reproduction. Updated originals are preserved under vendor/Starter_Kit_2026_11_10. New evaluations default to the updated two-leg runner; use --engine-format legacy only to reproduce an old-format experiment.
